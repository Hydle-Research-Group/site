Certified-AIF (AIF) is a new certification created by HRG that can be applied to any project or organization- at no cost.

If you're curious, visit our [AIF page](/aif), in which we've outlined everything to know about the AIF. 

## What & Why AIF?

Hydle Research Group does not discriminate against the use of AI, in fact, we allow the use of it ourselves. However, in what one may call "the era of AI", it is becoming significantly harder to determine if a
project was machine or hand written. 

The AIF certification was created in hopes that, people can explicitly flag "this project was created entirely by ME." 

Since organizations are permitted to be entirely AIF, AIF creates a unique stamp that labels organizations run by humans, not AI.

_Under these words, HRG is not an AIF organization._
