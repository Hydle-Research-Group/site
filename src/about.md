Hydle Research Group (HRG for short) is an independent research organization started by Keller Hydle, an electrical engineering major from Colorado.

HRG is involved in the study and construction of all systems. Rather than limiting ourselves to one domain, researchers and engineers study the concepts they are curious about. This research ranges all the way from 3D Animation to Robotics, and we publish articles regularly on the [news page](/news/).

Additionally, HRG is dedicated to doing anything we can in-house. We maintain a [parts database](https://github.com/Hydle-Research-Group/manufacturing/) for our own design & manufacturing, along with our website tool, [Insite](https://hydle-research-group.github.io/insite/).
