# Launching Hydle Research Group

Announcing the founding of Hydle Research Group 

*June 6th, 2026*

<hr>

Today, we are proud to announce the founding of **Hydle Research Group**, an independent organization dedicated to advancing research and engineering across computer science, robotics, and aerospace.

Hydle Research Group was founded with a simple mission: build ambitious technology, pursue difficult problems, and create systems that push beyond conventional boundaries. Our work will focus on both theoretical and applied research, with projects ranging from software systems and embedded electronics to robotics and near-space aerospace platforms.

We believe the future will be shaped by small, highly capable teams willing to experiment, iterate rapidly, and learn publicly. Hydle Research Group exists to become one of those teams.

Some of the areas we plan to explore include:

- Aerospace systems and high-altitude balloon platforms
- Embedded systems and low-level software engineering
- Robotics and autonomous control systems
- Computer graphics and next-generation software platforms
- Research tooling, simulation, and experimental infrastructure

This launch marks the beginning of a long-term effort. Over the coming months, we will publish technical write-ups, development logs, experimental results, and updates as projects evolve.

We are excited for what comes next.

Welcome to Hydle Research Group.
